Broken Stones Privacy Policy
Effective November 24, 2026

Broken Stones Studio operates the Broken Stones Discord bot. This policy explains the information the bot uses, why it uses it, and how you can request deletion.

Information we use
Discord account and server information. The bot uses Discord user, server, channel, role, and message IDs as needed to identify players, operate games and panels, and apply server settings. Display names and avatars may appear in profiles, games, or leaderboards.
Gameplay and settings. The bot stores information needed to keep your progress and run games. This includes character and pet progress, inventory, currency, rewards, leaderboards, and server settings chosen by admins.
Messages and feature inputs. The bot processes messages in features that need them, such as the Counting Game and Global Chat. Global Chat relays messages to other participating servers. Oracle’s Keep uses information you choose to enter, such as your first name, birth date, or question, to provide the selected feature.
Supporter information. If you use supporter benefits, the bot may process membership status, tier, linked Discord account information, and claim records to verify and deliver those benefits. External payment platforms handle payment details under their own privacy policies.
How information is used and shared
We use this information to operate the bot, save progress, deliver rewards and supporter benefits, troubleshoot problems, and prevent abuse. Depending on the feature, other players may see your display name, gameplay results, leaderboard entries, or messages you send to Global Chat. Server admins can access settings and information shown by the bot’s admin tools.

Discord provides the messaging platform, Render hosts the bot, and Patreon supports optional memberships. Each provider handles information associated with its service under its own policies. We may disclose information when required by law.

Retention and deletion
We retain gameplay records and server settings as needed to maintain progress and operate the bot. Removing the bot from a server does not automatically delete a player’s cross-server progress.

To request access, correction, or deletion, contact Broken Stones Support privately and include your Discord user ID. Server owners can also request deletion of their server’s saved bot settings. We may ask you to confirm that you control the relevant account or server. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or identity documents.

For a verified deletion request, we will delete the relevant data under our control unless applicable law requires us to retain it. Relayed messages may still exist in Discord channels, and Discord and external providers handle deletion of their own records.
